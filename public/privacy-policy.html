Digital Saathi Privacy Notice

Please read this notice before submitting the learner registration form.

1. Information we collect

The registration form may collect your name, age, phone number, village or city, learning goal, current experience level and the date of registration. Age, phone number, location and learning goal are optional.

2. Why we use the information

The project team uses these details to understand learning needs, organise digital literacy activities and contact a learner about the programme when contact details are provided.

3. Who can view the information

Registration details are available to authorised Digital Saathi project administrators through the protected admin dashboard. Do not submit information belonging to another person unless you are authorised to do so.

4. Storage and retention

Registration details are stored in the project's PostgreSQL database. Before launch, the project team must add the approved retention period here (for example, a specific date or a defined period after the programme ends) and securely delete records when that period ends, unless there is a documented reason to retain them longer.

5. Your choice

Submitting the form is voluntary. You may choose not to submit it. To request correction or deletion of a registration, contact the project administrator using the contact details supplied by the project team.

6. Important safety reminder

Never enter your UPI PIN, ATM PIN, bank password, OTP, card details or other banking secrets in this form. Digital Saathi will never ask for them.

7. Project contact

Before publishing this website, the project team must add an active contact email address or phone number here so learners can ask questions or make a privacy request.
